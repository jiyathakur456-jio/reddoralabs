Annexure A

Terms & Conditions of Internship

1. Role & Responsibilities

You have been appointed as an Event Management Intern in the Events & Partnerships team. Your Reporting Manager and Mentor during this internship will be Mr. Prem Patil (Founder & CEO). Your primary responsibilities will include but are not limited to:

  • Planning and coordinating cybersecurity workshops, bootcamps, and webinars
  • Outreach to colleges, universities, and organizations for event partnerships
  • Managing event logistics, registrations, and attendee communications
  • Coordinating with speakers, sponsors, and guest participants
  • Promoting events across social media and email channels
  • Post-event reporting, feedback collection, and analytics
2. Duration & Commencement

The internship commences on 15 July, 2026 and concludes after a duration of 3 months (15 October, 2026), unless extended by mutual written agreement. During this period, you are expected to dedicate your time and efforts to RedDoraLabs. Any upcoming events, unavailability, or scheduling conflicts must be communicated to your mentor in advance for proper planning.

3. Work Structure & Schedule

This internship follows an assignment-based work model. There are no fixed working hours. Tasks and assignments will be communicated by your mentor on a regular basis, and you are expected to complete them within the mutually agreed deadlines. You have the flexibility to manage your own schedule as long as deliverables are submitted on time and quality standards are met.

The internship will be conducted in Remote mode. You are expected to remain responsive on designated communication channels (WhatsApp, Email, or project management tools) during reasonable hours and attend scheduled team meetings and review sessions.

4. Compensation & Payment

This internship follows a performance-based incentive model. There is no fixed monthly stipend. Instead, compensation is directly tied to measurable outcomes generated by your work. The structure is as follows:

  • Incentive Basis: When your content or efforts contribute to a measurable business outcome (e.g., a qualified lead, client conversion, or successful campaign), you will receive a percentage-based incentive from the revenue generated.
  • Payment Cycle: Incentives are calculated and disbursed on a monthly basis, within 15 days of the end of each calendar month, for all qualifying outcomes in that month.
  • Payment Method: All payments will be processed via PayPal to your registered PayPal account. You are responsible for maintaining an active PayPal account.
  • Transparency: A summary of qualifying outcomes and corresponding incentive calculations will be shared with you before each disbursement.

The specific incentive percentage will be communicated by your mentor on a case-by-case basis depending on the nature and value of the outcome. RedDoraLabs reserves the right to modify the incentive structure with prior written notice.

5. Intellectual Property

All work produced during your association with RedDoraLabs, including but not limited to written content, designs, strategies, documentation, code, and any intellectual property, shall be the exclusive property of RedDoraLabs. Sharing, storing, selling, distributing, or repurposing such work for personal use or third parties is strictly prohibited without prior written consent. You may reference your work in your personal portfolio with prior approval from your mentor.

6. Confidentiality & Data Privacy

Upholding data privacy and security is of utmost importance. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of any data, client information, business strategies, and contact details you may access during the internship. At the conclusion of the internship, you are required to:

  • Hand over all related data, files, and documents to RedDoraLabs
  • Delete all such data from your personal devices and storage
  • Revoke access to any shared accounts or tools

Breach of confidentiality may result in immediate termination and may attract legal consequences.

7. Exclusivity

During the internship period, you may continue your academic studies and coursework. However, engagement with any competing organization in the cybersecurity, AI, or content services space is not permitted without prior disclosure and written approval from RedDoraLabs.

8. Termination & Notice Period

Either party may terminate this internship with a notice period of 15 days under normal circumstances. RedDoraLabs reserves the right to terminate the internship immediately in case of misconduct, breach of confidentiality, non-compliance with Annexure A, or any behavior that damages the company's reputation or interests.
