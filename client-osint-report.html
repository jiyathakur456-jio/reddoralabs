1. Executive Summary

RedDoraLabs was engaged to conduct an Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) investigation to identify the individual behind a social media account that was used to post unauthorized photographs of the Client. The account was subsequently deactivated by its creator. The Client provided initial leads including partial email and phone number data obtained from the platform's password reset feature.

Through systematic OSINT analysis, our investigation has identified the suspected individual with High Confidence. The findings and supporting evidence are detailed below.

2. Case Background

Nature of Incident: Creation of a fake/impersonation social media account containing unauthorized photographs of the Client.

Account Status: Deactivated at the time of investigation.

Client's Initial Input:

  • Partial email address visible during password reset attempt (first/last characters)
  • Partial phone number visible during password reset attempt (last digits)
  • Pre-existing suspicion on a known individual

3. Methodology

The following OSINT techniques were employed during this investigation:

  • Email Pattern Analysis: Cross-referencing partial email with known email naming conventions and publicly available data
  • Phone Number Correlation: Matching partial phone digits against publicly accessible directories and linked accounts
  • Social Media Cross-Referencing: Analyzing connected/linked accounts, follower patterns, and account metadata
  • Digital Footprint Analysis: Examining publicly available records, profiles, and online presence
  • Behavioral Pattern Analysis: Assessing timing, content patterns, and account creation behavior

Tools Used: Publicly available OSINT frameworks, search engines, social media platform features, and reverse lookup services. No unauthorized access or illegal methods were employed.
