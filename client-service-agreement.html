1. Scope of Work

RedDoraLabs shall provide the following services to the Client:

  • Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) Investigation — identification of an individual or entity behind a specified online account/profile using publicly available information and ethical investigation methodologies.
  • Analysis of digital footprints, publicly available records, and open-source data to establish identity attribution.
  • Compilation of findings into a formal Investigation Report with supporting evidence.

2. Deliverables

  • Detailed OSINT Investigation Report containing findings, methodology used, and evidence collected.
  • Summary of identified digital identifiers (email patterns, phone linkages, account correlations).
  • Recommendations for further action, if applicable.

3. Timeline

The investigation shall be completed within 3-5 business days from the date of this agreement, unless additional time is required due to the complexity of the case. The Client will be informed of any delays in advance.

4. Confidentiality

RedDoraLabs shall maintain strict confidentiality of all information provided by the Client and all findings of the investigation. No data shall be shared with third parties without the Client's explicit written consent. All case files will be securely stored and retained for a period of 90 days after delivery, after which they will be permanently deleted unless otherwise requested.

5. Ethical & Legal Compliance

All investigation activities shall be conducted using legal and ethical methods only. RedDoraLabs exclusively uses open-source, publicly accessible information. No hacking, unauthorized access, social engineering, or illegal data acquisition methods will be employed. The Client acknowledges that results are based on publicly available information and may not be 100% conclusive.

6. Limitation of Liability

RedDoraLabs provides findings on a best-effort basis using publicly available data. We do not guarantee the completeness or absolute accuracy of results. RedDoraLabs shall not be liable for any actions taken by the Client based on the findings of this investigation. The Client is solely responsible for how they use the information provided.

7. Payment Terms

The fee for the services outlined in this agreement is detailed in the attached Invoice (RDL/INV/2026/001). Payment terms, applicable discounts, and final amounts are as specified in the invoice.
